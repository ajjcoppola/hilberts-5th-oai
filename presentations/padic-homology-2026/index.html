The ask (classical)

Can \(\mathbb{Z}_p\) act continuously and faithfully on a connected finite-dimensional manifold?

Finite \(C_{p^k}\) can (lens spaces). The obstruction must use the infinite descending chain of open subgroups.

1980: transfer

Over ℚ: (1/pʳ) ∑ g* is a projection ⇒ Ȟ*(X/ℤ_p; ℚ) ≅ Ȟ*(X; ℚ)^{ℤ_p} ⇒ no rational dimension jump Over ℤ / 𝔽_p: transfer ∘ projection = ×pʳ ≡ 0 ⇒ Smith–Gysin / Bockstein raises dim ⇒ Yang: dim_ℤ(M/ℤ_p) = n+2 (p-torsion)

classical Coppola 1980 · Yang · BRW

McAuley 2001 break

Orbit space dim \(n+2\) ⇒ saturated covers have order \(> n+1\).

To force unique \(n\)-cycles he uses \(U^m\) of order \(n+1\).

That cover cannot be \(A_p\)-invariant. Chain equivariance on the \(n\)-skeleton fails; the missing \((n+1)\)-chain is the transfer cocycle.

Theorem C — polyhedral

Connected polyhedral \(L\)-homology \(n\)-manifold ⇒ dense connected manifold stratum \(X\setminus|K^{(n-2)}|\).

Faithful \(\mathbb{Z}_p\) on \(X\) ⇒ faithful on a topological manifold.
