What's missing geometrically

The algebra is packaging. The geometry is a germ.

Quinn $I(X)$, OpenAI Witt characters, and Dabrowski $L_4$ tails look like different formalisms. Geometrically they all ask the same question:

Can a tiny continuous $\mathbb{Z}_p$-motion of a Euclidean (or ENR-dual) neighborhood manufacture a “unit’’ of duality — degree $1$ or signature $1$ — and then split that unit into $p^k$ equal covering pieces?

If yes for arbitrarily large $k$, integrality breaks. Sponges fail earlier: they never produce the unit.

What's missing geometrically

Two geometric moves (shared)

1. Chart compression $\to$ unit class. A small open subgroup of $\mathrm{Homeo}$ barely moves a ball. Haar-average / Newman rigidity $\Rightarrow$ a map that looks like the identity on a chart, then pinches to $S^d$ or $S^n\times\mathbb{CP}^2$. That map carries degree $1$ or signature $1$ (the “unit’’).

2. Finite cyclic covers $\to$ $p$-splitting. Approximate $\mathbb{Z}_p$ by $C_{p^k}$. A free (or free-on-detector) $C_{p^k}$-action gives an orbit map of covering degree $p^k$. Transfer / characters split the unit into $p^k$ equal pieces.

OpenAI writes (2) as Witt characters. Dabrowski writes (2) as $\tau=\mathbb{Q}[G/P]$ locally $p$ copies of $\mathrm{id}$. Same covering geometry.

What's missing geometrically

Why “characters’’ for both?

Not because Fourier analysis is the obstruction — because free finite cyclic actions are covers.
  • Orbit projection $E\to E/C_{p^k}$ has degree $p^k$
  • Transfer multiplies fundamental classes / signatures by $p^k$
  • Characters of $C_{p^k}$ are the Fourier language for the same isotypic splitting of equivariant forms
  • Dabrowski’s permutation module $\mathbb{Q}[G_i/P_i]$ is the chain-level cover: locally $p$ sheets

So OpenAI “equal parts $4/p^k$’’ and Dabrowski “eventually $p\mid\sigma$’’ are two bookkeeping systems for covering degree.

What's missing geometrically

Quinn $I(X)$: not “cobordism of $\mathbb{R}^n$’’

Common misread: “high-dimensional cobordism classifies Euclidean space.’’

More accurate:
  • An ENR $\mathbb{Z}$-homology manifold already has algebraic local Poincaré duality (like $\mathbb{R}^n$).
  • Resolution asks for a cell-like map from a genuine topological manifold — geometric charts up to cell-like collapse.
  • In dimensions $\ge 6$, surgery / $L$-theory says that obstruction is a single integer $I(X)$ (local symmetric signature of the duality “corner’’).
  • $I\equiv 1\pmod{8}$ is the Rokhlin-type shadow of $4$-dimensional signature phenomena inside that local $L$-class.

$I(X)$ measures: “does local duality come from manifold geometry, or only from homology?’’ — not global cobordism of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

How Quinn feeds the 2026 machines

On a true manifold, the unit in move (1) comes from a Euclidean ball.

On a nowhere-Euclidean ENR, there is no ball — but there is still local duality. The 2026 ENR rephrases treat the detector’s local signature as (a multiple of) $I(X)$:
  • OpenAI equal parts: $4I(X)/p^k$ vs lattice $L_d$
  • Dabrowski: local signature / tail linked to $I(X)$; single-$p$ cyclotomic fails when $p\mid I(X)$
Geometrically: Quinn replaces the missing chart’s unit by the local $L$-signature of the homology-manifold germ.

What's missing geometrically

So what was missing?

The missing geometric object is not a fancier invariant — it is the unit-producing germ:
  • a neighborhood that can supply degree/signature $1$ after tiny equivariant averaging, and
  • compatible free finite cyclic covers that split that unit by covering degree $p^k$.
Witt groups, characters, $L_4$ tails, and Quinn $I(X)$ are three languages for bookkeeping that germ + those covers.

Without the germ, Hilbert–Smith packaging does not start. With the germ but only single-$p$ arithmetic, exotic $I(X)$ divisible by $p$ can slip through (Dabrowski gap).

Preliminaries

What is a signature?

For an oriented $4k$-manifold $M$ (closed), the signature $\sigma(M)\in\mathbb{Z}$ is the signature of the intersection form $$ H^{2k}(M;\mathbb{Z})\times H^{2k}(M;\mathbb{Z})\to\mathbb{Z}. $$ Number of positive eigenvalues minus number of negative eigenvalues (after tensoring with $\mathbb{R}$).

Classic: $\sigma(\mathbb{CP}^2)=1$, $\sigma(S^4)=0$, $\sigma(M\#N)=\sigma(M)+\sigma(N)$.

Preliminaries

Signature as a bordism / surgery invariant

  • $\sigma$ is a cobordism invariant of oriented $4k$-manifolds.
  • In surgery theory, signatures (and their generalizations) obstruct whether a Poincaré complex is homotopy-equivalent to a manifold.
  • For group actions / controlled topology, one needs refined signatures: Witt classes, $L$-theory classes, or “tails” of ordinary signatures.

The 2026 HS arguments manufacture such a refined signature from a Euclidean chart + small $\mathbb{Z}_p$ subgroup, then get a contradiction with $p$-divisibility.

Preliminaries

Witt group (classical)

The Witt group $W(R)$ of a ring $R$ (e.g. $R=\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q},\mathbb{R}$) classifies nondegenerate symmetric bilinear forms up to metabolic (hyperbolic) summands.
  • $W(\mathbb{R})\cong\mathbb{Z}$ via signature
  • $W(\mathbb{Q})$ involves signatures at real and $p$-adic places
  • A class can be “integral” relative to a lattice / denominator bound

Witt OpenAI works in a sheafified / continuous Witt group of forms on $S^d$, not just a single number $\sigma$.

Preliminaries

Characters (for the OpenAI packaging)

A character of a finite abelian group $G$ is a homomorphism $\chi:G\to S^1$ (or $\mathbb{C}^\times$).
  • For $G=C_{p^k}=\mu_{p^k}$, characters are labelled by $p^k$-th roots of unity.
  • Haar averaging + character projection splits an equivariant class into isotypic / equal-size pieces.
  • OpenAI: $p^k$ equal character pieces of a Witt/signature class on the target sphere.

Slogan: characters cut the signature “pie” into $p^k$ equal slices.

Preliminaries

$L$-theory in one slide

Ranicki $L$-groups $L_n(R)$ are surgery obstruction groups for a ring (with involution) $R$.
  • $L_{4k}(\mathbb{Z})$ relates to ordinary signature
  • For group rings / asymptotic categories, $L_4(A_G(T))$ carries quadratic Poincaré data for a detector space $T$
  • “Ultimate” / Karoubi-localized $L$-theory forgets some control and yields ordinary integer signatures

Dabrowski uses $L_4$ of an asymptotic category, not a sheaf Witt group on $S^d$.

Preliminaries

Signature tail

A signature tail is a sequence $(\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\ldots)$ of ordinary integer signatures, considered modulo sequences that are eventually zero: $$ \sigma_{\mathrm{tail}}\;\in\; \mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}\big/\mathbb{Z}^{(\mathbb{N})}. $$ “Eventually $p$-divisible” means: for large $i$, $p\mid\sigma_i$.

Dabrowski’s contradiction: realization gives the constant tail $1$; divisibility says every tail is eventually in $p\mathbb{Z}$.

Preliminaries

ENR homology manifolds

An ENR (Euclidean neighborhood retract) $X\subset\mathbb{R}^N$ is a retract of some open neighborhood of itself.
  • $\mathbb{Z}$-homology $n$-manifold: $H_*(X,X\setminus\{x\};\mathbb{Z})\cong H_*(\mathbb{R}^n,\mathbb{R}^n\setminus\{0\})$ at every $x$
  • Compact ENR $\Leftrightarrow$ finite-dimensional ANR (roughly)
  • $X_{\mathrm{man}}$: set of points with a Euclidean chart $\cong\mathbb{R}^n$
  • BFMW: nowhere-Euclidean examples exist ($X_{\mathrm{man}}=\varnothing$)

Preliminaries

Resolution of homology manifolds

A resolution of an ENR $\mathbb{Z}$-homology $n$-manifold $X$ is a cell-like map $f:M\to X$ from a topological $n$-manifold $M$.
  • Edwards–Quinn: for $n\ge 6$, resolvability is detected by a single integer.
  • That integer is the Quinn index $I(X)$.
  • $I(X)=1$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $X$ admits a resolution.

If $I(X)=1$, then $X\times\mathbb{R}^2$ is a topological manifold — so Hilbert–Smith reduces to the manifold case.

Quinn index

Definition

For a compact connected ENR $\mathbb{Z}$-homology $n$-manifold $X$ with $n\ge 6$: $$ I(X)\;\in\;\mathbb{Z},\qquad I(X)\equiv 1\pmod{8}. $$
  • Constructed as a local symmetric signature of the Poincaré duality “corner” of $X$
  • $I(X)=1$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $X$ is resolvable (Edwards–Quinn)
  • Product formula: $I(X\times Y)=I(X)\,I(Y)$
  • BFMW construct examples with $I(X)\neq 1$ and $X_{\mathrm{man}}=\varnothing$

Quinn Local $L$-signature of the duality corner — geometric resolution obstruction, not a global cobordism class of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

Quinn index

Examples of values

  • Topological manifold / resolvable ENR: $I(X)=1$
  • Allowed congruence: $\ldots,\;-15,\;-7,\;1,\;9,\;17,\;25,\;\ldots$
  • Non-resolvable BFMW examples: e.g. candidates with $I(X)=9$ or $25$
  • If $I(X)\neq 1$, then $X\times Y$ is never a manifold for any generalized manifold $Y$ with $I(Y)\equiv 1\pmod{8}$ (product formula)

Quinn index

Why it matters for $\mathbb{Z}_p$

On a nowhere-Euclidean BFMW space there is no chart — Newman reduction fails.

Both 2026 arguments still hope to run on an ENR by treating the detector’s local signature as (a multiple of) $I(X)$:

Kill every $I(X)\neq 0$ $\Rightarrow$ no faithful $\mathbb{Z}_p$ on that ENR.

Resolvable case $I=1$: reduce via $X\times\mathbb{R}^2$ to a manifold first.

OpenAI packaging

Witt lattice on $S^d$

OpenAI Chart $\to$ odd-stabilize into $S^d$ $\to$ sheaf Witt / signature group $E_d(S^d)$.

Integrality: comparison lattice $L_d^{-1}\mathbb{Z}\,\overline{u}_d$ with fixed $L_d=2^{e_d}$ (independent of the $p$-power $k$).

Generator: a preferred class $\overline{u}_d$ (orientation / signature unit on the target sphere).

OpenAI packaging

Witt characters & equal parts

Haar-average a degree-one pinch through the orbit space. Character theory for $C_{p^k}$ splits the class into $p^k$ equal pieces: $$ \overline{z}_i(a) \;=\; \frac{4}{p^k}\,\overline{u}_d $$ On ENRs (rephrase): replace $4$ by $4I(X)$.

For large $k$ with $p^k > 4\,L_d\,|I(X)|$, this cannot lie in the comparison lattice unless $I(X)=0$.

Slogan: $p^k$ equal character slices vs a lattice with bounded denominators.

Dabrowski packaging

$L_4$ signature tails

Dabrowski Same obstruction family, different packaging.

Object: ultimate quadratic $L_4\bigl(A_G(T)\bigr)$ for a free $C_p$-detector $T$.

Tail: ordinary integer signatures in $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/\mathbb{Z}^{(\mathbb{N})}$.

Divisibility: every class has eventually $p$-divisible signatures (Karoubi / controlled Mayer–Vietoris; $\tau=\mathbb{Q}[G_i/P_i]$ locally $p$ copies of $\mathrm{id}$).

Realization: chart + Haar + $\mathbb{CP}^2$ germ $\Rightarrow$ a class with signature tail constantly $1$.

Hence $1\in p\mathbb{Z}$.

Dabrowski packaging

Cyclotomic detector (single $p$)

Lemma 3.1: one index-$p$ subgroup step uses $\Phi_{p^a}(1)=p$, so only single-$p$ divisibility of the tail / local signature.

On ENRs: if the local signature is Quinn’s $I(X)$ and $p\mid I(X)$ (e.g. $I=9$, $p=3$), then $I(X)\in p\mathbb{Z}$ fails to contradict.

Repair: $k$-step cyclotomic tower $\Rightarrow$ $p^k\mid I(X)$; choose $p^k > |I(X)|$ $\Rightarrow$ $I(X)=0$.

Takeaway

  • Geometry: unit-producing germ (chart or Quinn corner) + free $C_{p^k}$ covers of degree $p^k$.
  • Quinn $I(X)$: local $L$-signature measuring resolution — substitutes for a missing Euclidean ball on BFMW ENRs.
  • Characters / $\tau$: covering-degree bookkeeping (OpenAI Fourier language; Dabrowski permutation modules).
  • Algebra: integrality of Witt/$L$ tails vs unbounded $p$-splitting of the unit.

Same obstruction class — once you see the germ + covers, the three formalisms stop looking unrelated.
